# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want ClaimScout to be welcoming, respectful, and useful to people across journalism, research, education, civic technology, and open source.

Participants in this community are expected to:

- act in good faith
- be respectful and constructive
- assume positive intent before escalating
- critique ideas, not people
- avoid harassment, hate speech, intimidation, and personal attacks
- be careful when discussing misinformation so we do not accidentally amplify harmful content

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples include:

- harassment or discriminatory language
- doxxing or threats
- sustained disruption of discussions
- spam, manipulation, or coordinated brigading
- posting harmful misinformation as if it were verified fact
- hostile gatekeeping toward newcomers

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, demos, community calls, and any public spaces where people represent the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, reject contributions, or block participation when behavior harms the safety or usefulness of the community.

## Reporting

Please report serious issues privately to the maintainers. Replace the placeholder in `SECURITY.md` with your project email before launch.
